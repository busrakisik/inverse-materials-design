## Research question and why real outside input is now necessary

The V12.7–V12.10 outcome-free audits established that the true unit of an experimental intervention can be a coupon, a shared processing event, or an entire synthesis run; those distinctions affect independence and whether a processing contrast is algebraically estimable. Repeatedly generating improved artificial schedules cannot resolve the actual laboratory's feasible treatment-assignment unit.

**Candidate:** Fe–Co–Ni magnetic thin films, with composition/thickness controls, original magneto-optical Kerr hysteresis curves and diffraction-based structure/texture observations. Scientific explanation candidates are provisional and unapproved. Different coercive fields accompanied by different diffraction patterns would not by themselves prove a structure-mediated causal mechanism.

The first external decision is therefore **not** a sample count or optimizer result. It is a domain scientist's verifiable answer to whether any safe, independent and legally accessible intervention and relevant measurements are feasible.

## Frozen evidence classes, not invented scores

The machine-readable dossier enumerates only public historical facts and explicitly unresolved gates. Its status classes are:

- `institutional_inventory`: officially listed equipment/techniques; current operability and external accessibility unknown.
- `historical_research_example`: named prior work demonstrates past methods in a **different material system**; not capability certification for the proposed pilot.
- `institutional_directory`: published names, roles or public institutional contact route; not investigator consent.
- `conflicting_legacy_directory`: old contact metadata conflicts with later names/roles and must trigger a check.
- `unverified_prerequisite`: physically required feasibility/approval, **not evidenced** by any public webpage.

Only an external qualified investigator and authorized facility can independently resolve physical processing scope, legal permissions, funding, calibrated measurement capabilities, genuine sample independence, scientific hypotheses and safety. **No metadata field, web search, signature-like string, directory entry or Git commit can automatically promote a lead to verified feasibility.**

## Investigator-facing questions before a physical experiment

1. Can approved Fe–Co–Ni thin films of repeatable local composition and thickness be fabricated? If not, which feasible magnetic-film system would be scientifically more appropriate?
2. Which intervention can be assigned independently: individually to separate coupons after deposition, as a shared postprocessing run, or across entire deposition runs? Are treatment units physically independent, and what sources of common-mode noise remain?
3. Are calibrated MOKE hysteresis and raw XRD measurements currently accessible for those specimens? What field geometry, orientation, limits and trace-level uncertainty definitions are appropriate?
4. Can the same original specimen be linked to original composition/thickness observations and magnetic/structural traces, with unique identifiers, synthesis and processing batch history, instrument ID, timestamps and failed attempts?
5. What is the actual time/material cost of one independent manufacturing and characterization unit, and what sample/sample-loss constraints or safe process limitations apply?
6. Who can approve the experiment design, rights and safety? Is a supervised unfunded, credit-bearing or visitor research arrangement allowed, and if so by whom?
7. Can the lab predefine a meaningful coercive-field difference in A/m, reproducibility targets and realistic variance sources *before* prospective evidence acquisition?

The initial inquiry should request a **brief feasibility discussion**, not immediate performance evaluation, free experimental work, access to unpublished measurement data or physical execution.
